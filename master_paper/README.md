# Master Paper

This directory contains the central manuscript for the Induced Dependence scientific program.

## Purpose

The master paper should establish the general theory of induced dependence: its conceptual motivation, formal structure, explanatory scope, and relationship to existing scientific frameworks.

## Suggested Scope

- Core definition of induced dependence.
- Distinction between intrinsic dependence, observed dependence, and induced dependence.
- General mathematical or causal formulation.
- Cross-domain examples motivating the framework.
- Criteria for identifying induced dependence in empirical or simulated systems.
- Relationship to derived papers and domain-specific applications.

## Notes

This paper should serve as the conceptual anchor for the entire repository.

# Master Paper Outline

## Working Title

Induced Dependence: A General Framework for Context-Produced Dependency

## Central Thesis

Some dependencies are not fixed properties of isolated entities. They are induced by the structure of a system, by shared constraints, by observation, by intervention, or by the conditions under which components are made comparable.

## Abstract Sketch

This paper introduces induced dependence as a general framework for studying dependency structures that emerge from context, constraint, measurement, or intervention. The framework aims to unify patterns seen across physical systems, ecological networks, artificial intelligence observability, and mathematical models of dependence.

## Key Claims To Develop

- Dependence can be induced without being reducible to direct causation.
- Observation and intervention can change dependency structure, not merely reveal it.
- Shared constraints can produce systematic relations among otherwise independent components.
- A unified language for induced dependence may clarify several domain-specific puzzles.

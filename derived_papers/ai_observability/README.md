# AI Observability Paper

This directory contains the derived paper applying induced dependence to AI observability.

## Purpose

This paper should investigate how hidden dependencies in AI systems can become visible, distorted, or newly induced through monitoring, evaluation, intervention, or deployment context.

## Suggested Scope

- Observability in complex AI systems.
- Latent dependency structures in models, agents, or pipelines.
- Monitoring and evaluation as dependency-inducing processes.
- Failure modes caused by hidden or induced coupling.
- Practical implications for audits, interpretability, and safety.

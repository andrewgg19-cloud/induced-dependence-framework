# Mathematical Foundations Paper

This directory contains the derived paper focused on the formal foundations of induced dependence.

## Purpose

This paper should develop the definitions, assumptions, propositions, and possible theorem structure needed to support the broader scientific program.

## Suggested Scope

- Formal definitions of dependence and induced dependence.
- Candidate notation and mathematical objects.
- Relationship to probability, causality, information theory, graph theory, or dynamical systems.
- Conditions under which induced dependence appears, disappears, or changes form.
- Proof sketches, counterexamples, and minimal models.

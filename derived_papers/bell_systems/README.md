# Bell Systems Paper

This directory contains the derived paper applying induced dependence to Bell-type systems.

## Purpose

This paper should examine whether the induced dependence framework can clarify the structure of nonclassical correlations, measurement contexts, and dependency patterns in Bell-style scenarios.

## Suggested Scope

- Bell-type experimental structure.
- Measurement settings and observed correlations.
- Induced dependence as a lens on contextual or relational structure.
- Comparison with standard interpretations and mathematical constraints.
- Candidate simulations or diagrams for correlation patterns.
